Privacy

dino.game is a small, unofficial site for playing the T-Rex runner in a browser. This page explains what this site stores and measures. It is not legal advice, and it may be updated if the site changes.

High score on this device

Your high score and mute preference are saved in your browser’s localStorage under keys on this origin. Clearing site data in your browser will reset them. They stay on this device unless you accept analytics (see below).

Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-GYZFSQV2F8) only if you accept. Until you accept, we set Consent Mode so analytics_storage is denied, and we do not set _ga cookies or send hits to Google. If you accept, we may send anonymous events for a page view, starting a game, finishing a run (including that run’s score and the high score stored on this device), mute changes and fullscreen. Google may then set cookies or use similar identifiers as described in Google’s own documentation. We use this to improve the page, not to sell a list of players. You can reject analytics; we store that choice on this device and will not send those events.

Ads

There is a reserved space under the game for an advertisement, but it is empty until ads are separately approved. If ads are added later, this page will be updated, and any legally required consent will load ads only after that consent. The game itself will still play without an ad in front of it.

Cookies and the game

Playing the game does not require an account or a cookie wall. If you reject analytics, or block cookies, the runner still works; only measurement is skipped. You can change or withdraw that choice at any time with the Cookie settings link. Withdrawing consent sets Google Consent Mode to denied and deletes Google Analytics cookies (_ga) this site has set.
