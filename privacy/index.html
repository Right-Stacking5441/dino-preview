The short version

You can play without an account, and you don't have to accept any cookies to play. We only use Google Analytics if you click Accept. If you reject it, or never choose, the game works exactly the same and nothing is sent to Google Analytics.

What is stored on your device

Things the game needs. Your high score, your mute setting and your cookie choice are saved in your browser's local storage on this device. They are used only to run the game and remember your choice. No consent is needed for these because they are strictly necessary for things you've asked for. If you accept analytics, a finished run's score and your high score are included in the measurements described below. Clearing your browser's site data resets them.

Google Analytics cookies (only if you accept).

Name Set by Purpose Lasts
_ga dino.game (Google Analytics) Tells one browser apart from another, so we can count visitors 2 years

Until you accept, these cookies aren't set and the Google Analytics script isn't loaded.

What Google Analytics measures

If you accept, we measure page views, starting a game, finishing a run (including that run's score and the high score saved on your device), mute changes and fullscreen use, plus Google Analytics' standard automatic measurements, such as scrolling, clicks on links to other sites and time on the page. Google Analytics also receives technical details, such as your browser, device type, screen size, the page that sent you here, and an approximate location (country or city) worked out from your IP address. Google says Google Analytics 4 does not log or store IP addresses. We can't see who you are. We look at the results only as totals, to understand how the site is used and make it better.

We have turned off Google Signals, advertising features and Google's data-sharing settings, so this data isn't used for advertising or shared with other Google products.

Our legal basis

We use Google Analytics only with your consent (regulation 6 of the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations 2003 and, for visitors in the EU, the ePrivacy rules where they live). The personal data in it is processed on the basis of that consent (UK GDPR and EU GDPR Article 6(1)(a)). UK law now allows some analytics without consent, but we ask everyone, wherever they are, because that's the simplest and fairest approach.

Our host's server logs are processed on the basis of our legitimate interest in keeping the site secure and working (Article 6(1)(f)).

Changing your mind

You can withdraw consent at any time using the Cookie settings link at the bottom of every page. That switches analytics off straight away and deletes the Google Analytics cookies this site set. Withdrawing doesn't affect anything measured before you withdrew. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

Who receives your data

  • Google (Google Ireland Limited for UK and EU visitors, together with Google LLC in the US) provides Google Analytics. It acts as our processor under Google's data processing terms.
  • Netlify, Inc. hosts the site and keeps server logs (including IP addresses) for security and reliability, as our processor.

We don't sell your data or use it to show you personalised ads.

International transfers

Google and Netlify, Inc. may process data in the United States and other countries. Where data leaves the UK or the EU, it is protected by one of these: Google LLC's certification under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and its UK Extension (the UK-US data bridge); the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses; or the UK International Data Transfer Addendum, included in Google's data processing terms. Netlify's data processing agreement protects transfers using the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and, for UK data, the UK International Data Transfer Addendum to those clauses.

How long we keep it

Google Analytics event data is kept for [2 months], then deleted automatically. The totals and reports we've already produced may be kept longer, but they don't identify anyone. The cookies above last up to 2 years unless you delete them or withdraw consent. Netlify's CDN traffic and access logs, which include IP addresses, are kept online for 30 days and in archival storage for up to 13 months. Logs used to investigate security or abuse can be kept for longer, up to 6 years.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. If you're under 13, please ask a parent or carer before clicking Accept. Analytics stays off unless someone accepts it. We don't knowingly collect anything that identifies a child.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to restrict or object to its use, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time. Because we only hold pseudonymous analytics data, we may need details from you, such as your _ga cookie value, to find records relating to you. To use any of these rights, email [CONTACT EMAIL].

If you're unhappy with how we handle your data, please tell us first. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk, 0303 123 1113) or, in the EU, to your local data protection authority.
